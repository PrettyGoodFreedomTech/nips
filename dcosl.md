Discussion on the design of NIP-93: decentralized lists

NIP-93 is to form a basis for decentralized list formation. To gain acceptance by the nostr community, it will need to be as simple and straightforward as possible to achieve the stated goal. What the nostr community does not necessarily need to know at this point is that this NIP is also designed to form the basis of what will become, gradually, into the basis for the Concept Graph. In this article I will discuss the constraints that that imposes.

The Concept Graph breaks down information into small chunks called words, each word corresponding to one node in the Concept Graph. Each word is of one or more word types. There is a one to one mapping between a concept and a word type, and there are an unlimited number of word types, and the community may or may not agree on the list of all word types. Therefore, we cannot use a different event kind for each word type. Instead, NIP-93 will specify a word as either kind 9999 or kind 39999, depending on whether we want the word to be editable, and will use a single one-letter tag, the z tag, to specify one or more word types.

For starters, there will be only one word type: the list. So we could specify this as `[z, list]`. We may want to consider whether the NIP should specify `[z, list]` or `[z, "*"]`, the idea being that over time what we are calling a list will gradually morph into the idea of a wordType or a concept. Implications are that it needs a singular and a plural name. But Maybe we should start with list, and eventually list will be some sort of subset of word type, or maybe not a subset but they will share the characteristic of the format of name needing a singular and a plural. So maybe just use list instread of * for now.
